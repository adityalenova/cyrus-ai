// ground rules

How this community behaves

  • Stars are the only ranking. No paid placement, no self-promotion boost. Listing your own repo is fine — games don't work here.
  • Cite, don't hype. Forum claims about a tool should link to the repo, an issue, or a benchmark.
  • Licenses first. Submissions without a license get bounced, politely — it protects every downstream user.
  • Maintainers get thanked publicly and funded privately. The sponsor board exists so appreciation converts into sustainability.
